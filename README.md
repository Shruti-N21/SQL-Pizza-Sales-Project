# Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using SQL to understand overall sales performance, customer ordering patterns, pizza pricing, size preferences, and product demand.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The pizza business needs to understand its sales performance and customer ordering patterns to identify popular products, preferred pizza sizes, revenue performance, and opportunities to improve sales.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total pizza orders.
* Calculate total revenue.
* Identify the highest-priced pizza.
* Determine the most frequently ordered pizza size.
* Identify the most popular pizza types based on quantity.
* Generate insights that can support business and sales decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

**Tool:** SQL

**Skills:**

* Data Analysis
* Aggregate Functions
* GROUP BY
* ORDER BY
* Filtering
* Sorting and Ranking
* Business Analysis

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Pizza Orders

Calculated the total number of pizza orders placed.

**Result:**
**21,350 total orders**

### 2. Total Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated from pizza sales.

**Result:**
**$817,860.05 total revenue**

### 3. Highest-Priced Pizza

Analyzed pizza prices to identify the highest-priced product.

**Result:**
**Greek Pizza** was the highest-priced pizza.

### 4. Most Ordered Pizza Size

Analyzed order quantities by pizza size to understand customer preferences.

**Result:**
**Large (L)** was the most frequently ordered pizza size.

### 5. Top Pizza Types by Quantity

Ranked pizza types based on the total quantity ordered.

**Result:**
**Classic Deluxe Pizza** was the most ordered pizza type.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* The business generated more than **21K orders**.
* Total revenue exceeded **$817K**.
* Large pizzas were the most preferred size among customers.
* Classic Deluxe Pizza had the highest order quantity.
* Greek Pizza had the highest price among the pizzas analyzed.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business could:

* Promote popular pizza types such as Classic Deluxe Pizza.
* Consider combo offers around the most preferred Large size.
* Review the performance of lower-selling pizza types.
* Use sales trends to improve inventory planning.
* Evaluate pricing strategies for premium-priced products.

## 📌 Result

The analysis helped identify **overall revenue performance, customer ordering preferences, popular pizza sizes, highest-priced products, and top-selling pizza types**. These insights can support better decisions around **menu planning, inventory management, pricing, and sales strategy**.

## ✅ Project Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to analyze transactional sales data and convert raw order information into meaningful business insights. The analysis provided a clear understanding of customer preferences, product demand, pricing, and revenue performance, helping identify areas that could be explored further for improving sales and business operations.
